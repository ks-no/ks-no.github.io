This site documents the Fiks platform. Most of the documentation is in Norwegian. Only some services have an English version.

The documentation is for those who administer the platform on behalf of a municipality or other organization, or who develop integrations against the APIs Fiks offers.

#### Available in English

- [KS Besøksbidrag (visitor levy)](/en/tjenester/besoksbidrag/) – the Register API, and reporting for accommodation providers (coming)

#### Errors

If you find errors or something that is not described well enough, please send an email to [fiks@ksdigital.no](mailto:fiks@ksdigital.no).

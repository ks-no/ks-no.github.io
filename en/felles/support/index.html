Get help and support

For any questions about the Fiks services, you can contact us through the following channels:

Contact channels

  • Email: fiks@ksdigital.no
  • Slack: #fiks-support - See below for how to get access

GitHub

If you find a bug or have a suggestion for improvement:

  • GitHub Issues: Many of KS’s services and clients are open source. See github.com/ks-no for relevant projects

Getting access to Slack channels

If you are integrating against the Fiks platform and want faster dialogue with the developers, we recommend our Slack channels:

  1. Send an email to fiks@ksdigital.no
  2. Provide the email addresses of the people who need access
  3. We will invite you to the relevant Slack channels
